## Identity

Lynn Marie Kistler holds a B.S. in Physics from Harvey Mudd College and M.S. and Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Maryland (Ph.D. thesis, 1987: "A study of the energy spectra of the major ion species in the ring current region of the magnetosphere during geomagnetic storms"). As of the most recent UNH and Wikipedia records, she is Professor of Physics & Astronomy in the College of Engineering and Physical Sciences (CEPS) and Director of the Space Science Center within the UNH Institute for the Study of Earth, Oceans, and Space (EOS). She was elected a Fellow of the American Geophysical Union in 2016 for "... prolific seminal contributions to our understanding of the role of heavy terrestrial ions in magnetospheric structure and dynamics."
