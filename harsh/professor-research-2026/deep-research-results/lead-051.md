## Identity

IRAP (Institut de Recherche en Astrophysique et Planétologie) is UMR 5277, jointly operated by CNRS and Université Toulouse III–Paul Sabatier, with CNES as a third tutelle. It is the only French laboratory of this name and acronym in this discipline, so the identity does not collide with a namesake. The PEPS team ("Planets, Environments and Space Plasmas" / "Planètes, Environnements et Plasmas Spatiaux") is one of IRAP's research teams; the team page lists Vincent Génot as leader and Philippe Garnier and Jérémie Lasue as deputies. The published research themes (heliophysics, planetary magnetospheres, planetary plasma environments, plasma specification models for space missions) match the description supplied in the lead record.
