### Research Scope of the PWI Group

The group's stated strategic goal is "to carry out a strong experimentally-based program focused on ITER-relevant topics", covering:
- Lifetime of plasma-facing materials (PFM) and plasma-facing components (PFC)
- Tritium inventory accumulation in PFC
- Carbon and metal (Be, W) dust formation
- Material migration studies via tracer techniques and in-vessel diagnostics
- Development of fuel removal methods (photonic cleaning, laser, ICWC/ECWC)
- Surface analysis of plasma-facing components (nuclear microprobe, IBA)

The page lists routine participation in EUROfusion, ITPA, IEA, and IUVSTA frameworks, with collaborators at JET/UK, CEA Cadarache (WEST), Max-Planck IPP Garching, Forschungszentrum Julich (TOMAS), Uppsala University (ion beam analysis), and Czech Academy COMPASS tokamak.

Source: https://www.kth.se/emp/research/fusion/plasma-wall-interaction-pwi-1.37584
