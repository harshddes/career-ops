## Identity

James Connell (commonly "Jim Connell") is a Professor of Physics and Astronomy at the University of New Hampshire. He is faculty in the Space Science Center (SSC), a unit within UNH's Institute for the Study of Earth, Oceans, and Space (EOS), and his principal research interest is measuring energetic particle radiation in space, including Galactic cosmic rays, Solar energetic particles, and anomalous cosmic rays, using space-based instruments and detector development.
