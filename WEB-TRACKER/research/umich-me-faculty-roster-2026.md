## comparative_read_of_the_roster_5_second_takeaways_for_outreach

- **Lab/site footprint**: 8 of the 38 active faculty are linked to a named lab or research group URL on their official ME profile (Aruda, Awtar, Epureanu, Erinin, Filipov, Guo - not listed -, Kruger, Meyhofer, Moore). The remaining labs / centers are not named on the ME profile itself and require following the professor's homepage or departmental org to discover.
- **Optical + fluids cluster strength**: Ceccio + Erinin + Meyhofer combine for hot optical diagnostics inside experimental fluids - likely the most natural group of contacts for any cross-cutting optical / DAQ / vacuum-instrument partnership.
- **Aerospace hardware contact pair**: Epureanu (turbomachinery / aerospace structures) and Filipov (origami deployable structures / biomedical robotics) form the strongest space-adjacent structural ME pair.
- **Combustion-program pair**: Middleton (CFD-driven combustion researcher) + Sick (Global CO2 Initiative director, laser-combustion diagnostics expertise) cover the modeling and experimental sides of combustion diagnostics at ME.
- **Manufacturing / process monitoring bench**: Abell (weld monitoring), Kruger (Wu Center), Awtar (precision design), Shih (manufacturing), Epureanu (Automotive Research Center), and Freiheit (systems-level manufacturing research) together cover the breadth from process monitoring to systems-level manufacturing.
- **Sociology side**: Only Cech represents engineering-workforce / culture research; her ME affiliation is joint with Sociology, and she is not relevant to physical instrumentation prospecting.
