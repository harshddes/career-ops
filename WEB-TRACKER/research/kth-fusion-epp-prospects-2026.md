## FP3 / EUROfusion Collaborators

### Chalmers University of Technology (Department of Electromagnetics)

The Plasma Control page names Chalmers as the theory group that KTH's EXTRAP T2R programme collaborates with on Resistive Wall Mode (RWM) modelling, and a public PowerPoint on T2R "Intelligent shell" experiments names D. Gregoratto, Y. Liu, and A. Bondeson as the Chalmers theory group members of that collaboration. Sources: [4_fp3_eurofusion_style_collaborators_verifiable_on_kth_pages[0]] [34]; [4_fp3_eurofusion_style_collaborators_verifiable_on_kth_pages[1]] [52].

Mechanism: Chalmers provides MHD / RWM theory while KTH's EXTRAP T2R serves as the flexible experimental test bed. Implication: for an experimental diagnostics project, a co-supervisor at Chalmers could be Lienard or Bondeson, but a formal KTH-side open position is the entry point.

A 2026 KTH page also lists Per Helander as a Chalmers alumnus / adjunct (2002-2005) who later led theory at IPP Garching ([4_fp3_eurofusion_style_collaborators_verifiable_on_kth_pages[2]] [89]) and Tünde Fulop is the current Chalmers professor in plasma physics ([4_fp3_eurofusion_style_collaborators_verifiable_on_kth_pages[3]] [91]). Neither is on the KTH page itself; they are EU-wide FP-class peers.

### Uppsala University (FREIA and Tandem Lab)

The PWI page names Uppsala University's Tandem Accelerator facility as the source of "several ion beam based analysis methods" that KTH PWI uses for post-exposure analysis of material samples retrieved from EXTRAP T2R. Source: [4_fp3_eurofusion_style_collaborators_verifiable_on_kth_pages[4]] [56] (linking to https://www.tandemlab.uu.se/?languageId=1). The Tandemlab's own page describes an accelerator laboratory for C-14 dating and material research, with ion-beam induced modification and non-destructive characterisation. Source: [4_fp3_eurofusion_style_collaborators_verifiable_on_kth_pages[5]] [45].

Mechanism: PWI's materials diagnostics pipeline is KTH (in-vacuo insert / extract in EXTRAP) -> Uppsala Tandem Lab (ion-beam analysis). Implication: a SIMION / SRIM-aware candidate is exactly what the PWI - Tandem pipeline needs to plan ion-trace simulations ahead of beam time.

### Other EUROfusion / ITER-Linked Partners Named on KTH Pages

## Synthesis

Conflict / divergence surfaced across subgroups:
- Experimental-only (EXTRAP T2R, Plasma Control) groups vs modelling-heavy (Fast Electron Physics).
- Emeritus-led (PWI, Rubel) vs active mid-career (Frassinetti, Brunsell, Hoppe).
- Domestic Sweden (KTH <-> Chalmers <-> Uppsala Tandem) vs EU collaborators (RFX Padua, EUROfusion).

Mechanism (institutional): Swedish fusion is a closed-loop of three institutions (KTH experimental, Chalmers theory, Uppsala beam analysis), and KTH's own pages name this closed loop explicitly. Implication: a candidate with a mixed experimental + beam-analysis profile can hit three KTH-side subgroups with one supervisor match.

Mechanism (funding): PWI survives in part on EUROfusion / Euratom grants and on national VR funding; Fast Electron Physics explicitly cites EUROfusion / ITER / ITPEA on their page. Implication: candidates willing to write a EUROfusion Engineering Grant pitch with their supervisor (Rubel or Hoppe) can convert a modelling-heavy MS into a paid PhD even when no Varbi listing is open.

Mechanism (career risk): an emeritus PWI supervisor is a feature, not a bug, for a candidate who wants a clean diagnostics thesis - emeritus PIs at KTH have a track record of co-supervising younger PhDs. Implication: the bar is to pre-confirm a co-supervisor (Frassinetti or Brunsell) before committing.
