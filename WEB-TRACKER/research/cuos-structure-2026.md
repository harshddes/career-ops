# CUOS Structure Decode — University of Michigan (2026-07-13)

## One-sentence truth

**CUOS is a College of Engineering center (umbrella).** Graduate hiring is **not** one shared CUOS inbox — each research group / PI hires (or takes students) separately. The page that lists only Karl / Alec / Louise is the **High Field Science → Join Us** page, not a center-wide jobs board.

## What CUOS is

- Full name: **Gérard Mourou Center for Ultrafast Optical Science**
- Site: https://cuos.engin.umich.edu/
- Role: interdisciplinary optics/ultrafast center in Michigan Engineering
- Flagship facility under CUOS management: **NSF ZEUS** (https://zeus.engin.umich.edu/) — U.S. highest-power laser user facility
- Historic facility: **HERCULES** (high-field laser lineage feeding ZEUS)

## Hiring mental model (important)

| Layer | What it means for outreach |
|-------|----------------------------|
| Center (CUOS) | Shared facilities, seminars, brand. Not one HR pipeline for grad research. |
| Research group | Scientific theme + usually one PI or a small faculty cluster. |
| Individual PI lab | **Actual** student / staff decisions, funding, openings. |
| ZEUS user facility | External users via proposals; staff roles are facility-side, not “join CUOS PhD.” |

**Action:** email **each PI** whose group matches your work. Do not assume HFS replied for the whole center.
