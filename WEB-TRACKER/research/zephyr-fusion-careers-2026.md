## What they do

[Zephyr Fusion](https://zephyrfusion.com/) is a Y Combinator Fall 2025 startup building **in-orbit, megawatt-class fusion power** for the space economy. Thesis: space vacuum enables a **dipole magnetic confinement** approach that avoids the massive terrestrial reactor hardware scaling problem; combined with **HTS magnets** and lower launch costs, compact orbital fusion becomes economically plausible.

Sources: [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zephyr-fusion), [YC launch post](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Oox-zephyr-fusion-in-orbit-fusion-power), [DCD coverage](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/y-combinator-champions-startup-promising-space-based-nuclear-fusion-power).

**Fusion approach tags:** Levitated dipole, magnetic confinement (per industry trackers).

**Stage:** Pre-seed; team was ~2 at YC launch, now hiring founding engineers. Investors include YC, Alumni Ventures, Pioneer, Buckley Ventures, Unpopular Ventures, Nova Threshold, Zaka ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/zephyr-fusion)).

## Internship reality check

- **No formal internship program** posted.

- **Best path:** Short email to `founders@zephyrfusion.com` asking for a **paid or credit-bearing research internship** focused on diagnostics, DAQ, or test-rig support. Reference Galen's diagnostics background and your plasma instrumentation work.
- YC startups often hire interns who can ship lab/test infrastructure in 8–12 weeks ([startup internship outreach patterns](https://www.colinkeeley.com/blog/cold-email-your-way-into-an-internshipjob-at-any-startup)).

## Outreach strategy (cold email)

Based on [Colin Keeley](https://www.colinkeeley.com/blog/cold-email-your-way-into-an-internshipjob-at-any-startup), [HackUnited](https://blog.hackunited.org/cold-email-your-way-into-an-internship-or-job-at-any-startup), [Whali 2026 guide](https://whali.co.uk/blog/cold-email-internship-guide):

1. **Keep first email under ~150 words** — ask for a **15-minute conversation**, not a job offer.
2. **Personalize to thesis** — dipole-in-space, vacuum confinement volume, megawatt space power gap.
3. **One technical hook** — Galen's LLNL diagnostics work ↔ your CEM/ESA calibration + HV plasma-source test automation.
4. **Attach or link 2 proof points** — portfolio + SSD readout or ion-optics bundle.
5. **Send Tue–Thu morning** PT; follow up once after 5–7 days if no reply.
6. **Separate threads:** (a) intern inquiry, (b) founding electrical application with PDFs — or combine if you want one thoughtful note covering both.
