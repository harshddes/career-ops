## ITER Diagnostics Pipeline

The ITER Diagnostic Group under Meister develops ITER upper-port bolometers and ionisation pressure gauges; both designs trace back to IPP patents from the 1980s for ASDEX and ASDEX Upgrade [iter_diagnostics_group_bolometer_and_pressure_gauge_pipelines[0]] [12]. Bolometer instrumentation is the single highest-fit area for FPGA-based detector readout because the IPP bolometer-camera electronics are upgraded every experimental campaign and depend heavily on DAQ throughput, fast ADC sampling, and high-voltage biasing of the bolometer bridge resistors.

Implication: a candidate with vacuum/DAQ/high-voltage test expertise aligns directly with how IPP bolometer cameras are characterised and certified. Recommendation: in the cover email, reference ITER upper port design codes, bolometer-grade amplifier design, and the IPP bolometer group's published noise spectra to short-circuit the screening filter.

## NBI And Heating Architecture

Robert Wolf's directorship (since 2006) and his JET PhD make him IPP's senior figure for ITER-relevant heating and current drive [greifswald_nbi_and_heating_architecture[0]] [52]. The NBI sub-group under ITED, hosted jointly with Fantz's division, again leverages IPP's ELISE and BATMAN test stands (negative ion sources) and feeds directly into ITER's neutral beam heating line via the ITER Neutral Beam Test Facility contributions.

Implication: instrumentation work for NBI test stands (high-voltage isolation of the ion source, calorimeter DAQ, beam diagnostics) is a structured workstream at IPP, and Wolf is the senior refereeing voice for any PhD touching negative-ion beam technology. Recommendation: if the candidate's vacuum/HV background is the primary hook, lead with Wolf; if FPGA/SIMION particle-tracking is the hook, route through Firmenzent instead.

IPP routes PhD applicants through three parallel channels:

- **Direct IPP application route** (group-by-group): identify a PI from this map and email the PI directly with a CV + project proposal; supervisors co-register you at TU Munich (Garching) or Ernst-Moritz-Arndt University Greifswald. - **EUROfusion / EURAXESS route**: EUROfusion's research-grant portal lists Engineering Grants and PostDoc Grants routed through IPP's Garching and Greifswald sites EUROfusion Project; EURAXESS lists Germany research positions including IPP under EURAXESS Jobs.

Recommendation: send one cover email to Meister + one to Wolf within 10 days of opening a position; in parallel, list yourself on the EUROfusion engineering-grant portal because the dataset in the EURAXESS portal refreshes every quarter with new IPP openings.
