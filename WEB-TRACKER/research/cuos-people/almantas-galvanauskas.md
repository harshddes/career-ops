## Why contact (beyond HFS)

Separate lab from High Field Science. Active PhD cohort. Bridges fiber laser engineering to laser-plasma intensity regimes.

## Outreach angle

Laser systems / pulse contrast / high-intensity experimental reliability; ask about openings for students who can help build/measure high-power fiber CPA systems feeding plasma or secondary-radiation experiments.
