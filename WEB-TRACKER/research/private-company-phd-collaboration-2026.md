# Private Company PhD Collaboration Paths — Fusion / Plasma Diagnostics

**Research date:** 2026-06-28  
**Dashboard source id:** `private-co`  
**Purpose:** Track industrial PhD routes where enrollment is at a university but research is co-supervised or hosted with a private fusion company.

---

## Path model (how this differs from KTH / IPP)

| Step | University / institute path (KTH, IPP) | Private company path |
|------|----------------------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Identify professor + lab | Identify company + university partner ecosystem |
| 2 | Email supervisor / apply to graduate school | Apply to **university PhD programme** with industrial co-supervision |
| 3 | Funding via university salary/stipend | Funding via university + company sponsorship / CASE / CDT / FOSTER-style placement |
| 4 | Research on campus + facility access | Research split: university degree + company lab access (ST40, test rigs, diagnostics) |

**Rule:** Private companies rarely enroll you directly. The viable model is **university PhD + company collaboration**, not "PhD at Tokamak Energy Ltd."

## Tokamak Energy — Industrial PhD Sponsorship

**Company:** Tokamak Energy Ltd (Oxfordshire, UK)  
**Spin-out from:** UKAEA / Culham Centre for Fusion Energy (2009)  
**Facility:** ST40 — world's highest-field spherical tokamak (Milton Park, Abingdon)  
**Careers contact:** careers@tokamakenergy.com

### Official programme

From [Early Careers — PhD Sponsorships](https://tokamakenergy.com/early-careers-fusion-energy-apprenticeships-student-placements-phd-sponsorship/):

- Tokamak Energy works with **UK and international universities** on postgraduate opportunities in science and engineering.
- **Applications are made to universities directly**, not to Tokamak Energy.
- Each year they host PhD research opportunities in collaboration with their scientists and engineers.
- Summer placements also exist via **FOSTER** (Fusion Opportunities in Skills, Training, Education and Research) with UKAEA — applications typically open in spring.
- Placement focus areas include: **diagnostics, plasma physics, materials, machine learning, experimental data analysis**, electrical systems, chemical engineering.

### Relevant UK university ecosystems

| Partner ecosystem | Why it matters for Harsh |
|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **University of York Fusion CDT** | Explicit pipeline to UKAEA, Tokamak Energy, First Light Fusion |
| **UKAEA / Culham / Oxford area** | Tokamak Energy HQ near Oxford; UKAEA spin-out heritage |
| **EPSRC Fusion CDT network** | Industrial co-supervision model common in UK fusion CDTs |
| **FOSTER + UKAEA** | Summer placement bridge before formal PhD application |

### Diagnostics fit for Harsh

Strongest overlap topics at Tokamak Energy:

- Magnetics diagnostics (see open role: Senior Instrumentation Engineer — Magnetics Diagnostics)
- ST40 plasma measurement, experimental data analysis
- Vacuum / cryogenic / high-field test hardware
- Space-plasma instrumentation transfer: HV-safe testing, DAQ, detector calibration, ion optics

### Application strategy

1. **Short term:** Email careers@tokamakenergy.com with CV; ask which university programmes or sponsored topics align with plasma diagnostics / experimental instrumentation (speculative outreach draft exists in `output/tokamak-energy/`).
2. **Medium term:** Apply to UK fusion PhD programmes (York CDT, Oxford/Culham-linked groups, Imperial, etc.) and name Tokamak Energy collaboration interest in research statement.
3. **Parallel:** Watch FOSTER summer placement announcements (spring) as a credibility bridge.

### Viability verdict

**Worth pursuing — yes, with correct framing.**

- Do **not** ask "can I do a PhD at Tokamak Energy?"
- Do ask "which university partnerships host diagnostics-focused industrial PhDs with ST40 access?"
- This is a **credible third path** alongside direct KTH and IPP professor outreach.
