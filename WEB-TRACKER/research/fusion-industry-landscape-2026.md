## Executive Summary

The fusion industry has crossed **$10B in total private investment** as of Q4 2025, with **45+ companies** commercializing fusion globally and workforce growth of **34% year-over-year** (4,000+ employees across the sector). Multiple companies are transitioning from R&D to hardware construction, creating unprecedented demand for instrumentation, diagnostics, and test engineers — exactly the skills transferable from space plasma physics.

**Your strongest overlap areas:** detector physics, HV test rigs, plasma diagnostics instrumentation, FPGA readout chains, ion-optics simulation (SIMION/SRIM), and calibration workflows. These map directly to fusion diagnostics roles.

**Critical constraint:** F-1 OPT visa → need employer willing to sponsor H-1B. Several top fusion companies have active H-1B sponsorship records. National labs and defense-adjacent companies (SHINE, some GA work) often require US citizenship — those are generally off-limits.

### Key Insight for F-1 OPT Strategy

Your M.Eng in Space Systems Engineering qualifies as a **STEM degree**, giving you **36 months of OPT** (12 months standard + 24-month STEM extension). This means:
- You can work for up to 3 years on OPT without H-1B

- H-1B lottery typically filed in March for October start
- **CFS, TAE, Helion, Pacific Fusion, and Inertia are your best bets** — large enough to have immigration counsel and enrolled in E-Verify
