## What the lab does

THz / microwave **nanoelectronics and nanophotonics** using carbon nanotubes, graphene, and related nanomaterials: high-frequency nano-devices, photovoltaics, chemical/biological sensors, nanomaterial synthesis. Uses THz techniques to probe carrier transport in nanostructures.
