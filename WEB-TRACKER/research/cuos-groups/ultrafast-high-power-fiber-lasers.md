## What the lab does

Three thrusts:

1. Novel fiber components (e.g. **Chirally Coupled Core / CCC** fibers)  
2. Advanced fiber laser systems (power/energy scaling, arrays, coherent combining, CPA)  
3. Applications (fiber-laser driven **EUV**, hard **X-ray**, high-intensity laser–matter work)

**Current ECE research emphasis (important):** high-power ultrafast fiber lasers for nonlinear optics, high-intensity laser–matter interactions, laser-driven secondary radiation (gamma/X-ray), and **laser acceleration of charged particles** — “new generation of **laser-plasma accelerator drivers**.”

Active PhD students (lab site, 2026): Mingshu Chen, Lauren Cooper, Chris Pasquale, Yanwen Jing, Tayari Coleman, Yu Bai, Bohan Zhou, Michael Garner, Bowei Yang, Jack Crandall — signals a living group.
