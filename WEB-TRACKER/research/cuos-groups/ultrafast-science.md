## What the lab does

Ultrafast lasers for **time-domain measurements** in condensed matter:

- Carrier dynamics in semiconductors / quantum dots / graphene / nanoplasmonics  
- THz generation, sensing, time-reversal imaging  
- Acoustic-phonon / nanostructure imaging  
- Biomedical imaging and in-vivo sensing (links to CUOS “Optics for Sensing and Nanomedicine” theme)

Historical CUOS director role (succeeded Mourou era); still listed as Ultrafast Science contact on CUOS Contact page.
