## What this group is

World-leading **relativistic laser–plasma** group inside CUOS. This is the wing that runs the ultra-intense laser program (HERCULES → ZEUS). Topics include laser wakefield acceleration, ion acceleration, radiation / neutron production, magnetized plasmas, laboratory astrophysics, theory/computation, and laser technology development.
