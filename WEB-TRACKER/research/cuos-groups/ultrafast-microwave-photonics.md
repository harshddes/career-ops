**URL:** https://cuos.engin.umich.edu/researchgroups/ultrafast-microwave-photonics/

## What the page describes

RF / microwave / mm-wave / THz electromagnetics combined with optics and ultrafast lasers: electro-optic / magneto-optic probes, near-field EM measurement, pulsed THz ranging/imaging/spectroscopy.
