## What the lab does

Ultrafast **laser–material interaction**: ablation and collateral damage in metals/semiconductors; pump–probe ultrafast microscopy; femtosecond LIBS (fsLIBS); dual-pulse LIBS; SEM/TEM and in-situ probes; ultrafast-laser nano/microfluidic channel manufacturing; epitaxial/thin-film growth (MBE) and surface science.
