- **Mahesh leads large-scale CFD and LES for naval flows**: Krishnan Mahesh (Richard B. Couch Professor) anchors the department's high-fidelity CFD capability (LES, RANS, ship and propulsor hydrodynamics) -> partner for CFD-heavy marine and offshore projects.

## synthesis

The NAME research-active roster organizes around five mutually reinforcing clusters:
1. Experimental hydrodynamics anchored at MHL (Maki as Director, with Dowling, Ganesh, Smith, McCoy, Collette, McKenney as users and program leads).
2. Computational hydrodynamics and CFD (Mahesh flagship LES; Dong free-surface/CFD; Wei and Naik on the research-scientist track; Martins adjoint MDO; Maki CFD-experiment integration).
3. Marine renewable energy (Bernitsas VIVACE; Zuo vibration harvesting - the two energy-harvesting thrusts).
4. Marine power, propulsion, and structures (Ceccio multiphase/propulsors; Hofmann power electronics/electric drives; McCoy MEL applied power; Pan combustion/propulsion; Collette MSDL structures; McKenney maritime decarbonization and ship design).
5. Controls and autonomy (Ghaffari CURLy; Sareen marine CPS; secondary inputs from Hofmann and Martins).

Cross-cutting tensions worth flagging for prospecting:
- The department's flagship director (Maki/MHL) is primarily experimental while the flagship tenured PI (Mahesh) is primarily computational; collaborations cross between them via the MHL-and-CFD pipeline.
- Affiliated Faculty from EECS (Hofmann) and Aerospace (Martins) bring non-marine-primary expertise (power electronics, MDO) into NAME, expanding what counts as marine power and yacht/offshore-design optimization.
- McKenney (Engineering Practice) and McCoy (Adjunct with MEL) are not classical research-track faculty but are research-active and central to applied ship design and marine power work.
- The Research Scientist track (Ganesh, Naik, Wei) supports the PIs operationally - their inclusion is critical for near-term prospecting because they often do day-to-day CFD, test prep, and analysis.
- Recent growth includes three early-career tenure-track hires (Pan, Sareen, Ghaffari) that all touch experimental or computational hydrodynamics, signaling a generational shift toward autonomy, propulsion physics and applied design.
