### Pfeiffer Vacuum (Asslar, Germany — global HQ; US service sites)

- **What they do:** Leading vacuum pump, gauge, and leak-detection OEM; part of Busch Vacuum Solutions group (~4,000 employees).
- **Why fit:** Direct vacuum technology; service and test roles use scopes, meters, troubleshooting electromechanical systems — mirrors your lab instrumentation work.
- **Careers:** https://jobs.buschvacuum.com/pfeiffervacuum  
- **Work-auth:** US roles (Nashua NH, San Jose CA, Austin TX) — H-1B status unknown; large multinational, ask early. Germany roles need EU work authorization.
- **Roles found:** Service Technician (Nashua), Field Service Engineer (Bay Area), Key Account Customer Quality Engineer (San Jose), Konstrukteur (Asslar), Student initiative (Asslar).

### VAT Group AG (Haag, Switzerland)

- **What they do:** World-leading high-performance vacuum valves and sealing; ~3,200 employees; semiconductor and R&D customers.

- **Work-auth:** Switzerland/EU permit required; not H-1B.
- **Roles found:** Lab Development Engineer (Haag), Electrical Development Engineer (Haag).
